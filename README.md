# 🎧 AudioTrack

**Where music meets company**: 
synchronized listening, mood-driven soundscapes, and dedicated playlists for every moment.

---

## ✨ What is AudioTrack?
**AudioTrack** is a real-time collaborative music web application that lets you share the exact beat with friends no matter where you are. Create a room, sync your playback down to the millisecond, and experience music together. 

---

## 🚀 Core Features

*⚡ **Live Room Sync**: Real-time synchronized listening sessions powered by WebSockets so you and your friends never miss a drop.
*🎭 **Core Moods**: Dynamic themes and song suggestions that shift your vibe across 
                     Lofi, Romantic, Sad, Chill, Happy, Energetic, Focus, Party and 
                     **Seasonal Specials** Summer, Rainy, Winter, Festival, Autumn.
*🔮 **Vibe Blending**: Mix and match two distinct moods to generate unique hybrid playlists tailored to your exact atmosphere.
*💌 **Dedication Playlists**: Send special song dedications to friends for birthdays or milestones that automatically auto-generate a curated `Dedication` playlist on their profile.
*📂 **Custom Playlists**: Build, organize, and manage your personal song collections effortlessly.
*🎨 **Dynamic UI**: Immersive background themes that adapt in real-time based on your selected mood.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

*   Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript
*   Backend: Node.js, Express.js
*   Real-Time Sync: Socket.IO
*   Database: MongoDB
